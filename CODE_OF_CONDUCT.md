# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Contributors, maintainers and users should collaborate constructively, give actionable feedback, credit others' work, and prioritize the safety and privacy of people affected by healthcare software.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and professional; disagree about ideas without personal attacks.
- Include precise, reproducible and **synthetic-only** examples when reporting problems.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses and project security boundaries.
- Accept good-faith review, correction and decisions about the project's scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized harassment, doxxing, impersonation, retaliation, targeted disruption, and posting credentials, PHI or confidential information are prohibited.

## Reporting and enforcement

For general community concerns, use a **private maintainer communication channel** associated with the repository; do not expose personal information in public issues. The maintainers have not yet published a dedicated confidential conduct-reporting email, and confidential reporting capability must be **confirmed before public launch**. Security vulnerabilities should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not ordinary issues.

Maintainers may moderate discussions, request changes, remove content or contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation after a proportional review. Reports should be assessed discreetly and without retaliation. No response SLA is promised.

## Scope

This policy applies to project-managed GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, documentation and release community spaces. It does not override applicable laws or the [Apache-2.0 license](LICENSE).
