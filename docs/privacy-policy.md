# Privacy and local data handling

> **Synthetic-data developer alpha only.** Do not enter patient information, clinical records or secrets.

HealthQualify's default Local Edition saves SQLite application data on the user's own machine and requires no hosted LLM account or paid AI tokens. Local storage may include usernames, password verifiers, sessions, synthetic qualification runs, evidence references and QA review records. Optional local Qwen is not activated unless the operator explicitly enables it and requests an explanation. External downloads, dependencies, GitHub and separately installed inference services have their own independent behavior.

Read the complete and maintained policy: **[PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Tec-Wel/HealthQualify/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)**.

For safe deletion, backups, networking exceptions, and limitations of the current privacy claims, follow that file; local-first is **not** a guarantee of audited privacy or HIPAA compliance.
