# HealthQualify trademark and brand-use policy

**Status:** interim project branding guidance. This policy does not claim that a trademark is registered, that a particular person owns the mark, or that any trademark search/clearance has been completed.

## Software license versus brand identity

The project software is licensed under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE). Section 6 does not grant general rights to use a licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names, except for reasonable and customary origin descriptions and reproduction of applicable notices. This policy supplements that distinction; it does **not** reduce software rights under Apache-2.0.

## Descriptive uses

Accurately identifying HealthQualify as the original software used, linking to its authentic repository, reproducing required license notices, and making truthful attribution statements are generally acceptable, subject to applicable law. Do not suggest approval, sponsorship, partnership, certification, or endorsement without authorization.

## Brand uses requiring a separate review

Please request written authorization from the verified rights holder before using HealthQualify logos or visual identity as the primary branding of a separate product, presenting an unofficial fork as an official distribution, registering confusingly similar domain names or project identities, or using the marks for merchandise or implied endorsements.

Forks remain permitted by Apache-2.0: use a distinguishable name/visual identity where necessary to prevent confusion, while preserving legally required copyright, origin, and license notices.

## No third-party endorsement

No affiliation with or endorsement by FDA, standards organizations, healthcare institutions, model vendors, or other open-source projects is claimed. Product copy should avoid unnecessary competitor comparisons, inspiration claims, borrowed trademarks, screenshots and logos. Do **not** remove legally required third-party attributions, regulatory references, dependency licenses, or factual technical links.

## Rights holder and permission channel

The legal owner of the HealthQualify mark and an authorized permissions contact are **not verified or published**. Maintainers must determine these before turning this guidance into an enforceable owner-specific trademark program or representing any mark as registered. No legal right is invented by this document.
